I cannot log into your itch.io account from here. Upload this zip yourself.

Use the file **A-Story-as-Old-as-Time-itch.html5.zip** (index.html at the zip root).

## Create the page

1. Open [itch.io/game/new](https://itch.io/game/new) while signed in.
2. **Title:** A Story as Old as Time
3. **Project URL:** `a-story-as-old-as-time` (or whatever is free)
4. **Short description:** Hunt the wilderness of Thornvale. Six callings, Tibia-style levels, three lives.
5. **Classification:** Games
6. **Kind of project:** HTML
7. **Release status:** Released (or In development)

## Pricing (the $3 donate)

1. Under **Pricing**, choose **$0 or donate**.
2. **Suggested donation:** `3` (USD). Players can skip and still play, or pay more.
3. Leave **Revenue share** at the default 10%, or drop it if you prefer.

To take money, connect PayPal or Stripe in [itch.io/user/settings/payouts](https://itch.io/user/settings/payouts) after the page exists.

## Upload

1. **Uploads** → choose `A-Story-as-Old-as-Time-itch.html5.zip`
2. Tick **This file will be played in the browser**
3. Embed options:
   - Display: **Click to launch in fullscreen**
   - Tick **Mobile friendly**
   - Leave **Click to play** on (audio starts after a tap)
4. Cover image: `cover.png` in this Drive folder (optional)

## Tags

Genre: Role Playing  
Tags: `rpg`, `2d`, `pixel`, `fantasy`, `action-rpg`

Save, then **View page** and tap Run game.
