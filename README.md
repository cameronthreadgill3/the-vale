# A Story as Old as Time — The Vale

Thornvale. Six paths. Hold Strike. The square finds the nearest foe.

Play: [vale-as-old-as-time.vercel.app](https://vale-as-old-as-time.vercel.app)

Book One stays free.

## What is here

- Email sign-up, four character berths, six classes
- Thornhearth town, shops, bank, training posts
- Hold-to-strike combat, skills around the button
- Realm chat, Who, death drops, equipment ladder

## Play

Walk west of the fountain into Nettle Copse. Hold Strike. Tap a beast to lock it. Skills sit around Strike (keys 1–4).
